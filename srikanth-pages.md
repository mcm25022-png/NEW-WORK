The film highlights the importance of determination, education, and self-confidence. ## PAGE 2: FILM STORY PLOT

### Film Story Plot

The story follows the life of Srikanth Bolla, who is born visually impaired in a small village. From childhood, he faces discrimination from society and even from educational institutions. People often underestimate his abilities because of his disability. However, Srikanth remains determined to study and achieve his goals.

As he grows up, he performs exceptionally well in academics and dreams of studying science. Despite facing rejection from various institutions, he continues to fight for his rights. Later, he gets the opportunity to study abroad and gains higher education. After returning to India, he starts a company that provides employment opportunities to differently-abled people. The film shows his struggles, failures, and achievements, ultimately presenting a powerful message that hard work and confidence can help overcome any obstacle.

## PAGE 5: IMPORTANCE OF THE FILM FOR INDIAN AUDIENCES

### B) Importance of the Film for Indian Audiences

Srikanth is an important film for Indian audiences because it raises awareness about the challenges faced by people with disabilities. It encourages society to treat everyone equally and focus on a person's abilities rather than limitations. The film promotes the values of education, perseverance, and self-belief.

The movie also inspires young people to work hard towards their goals despite difficulties. It shows that success is possible when a person remains determined and confident. For Indian audiences, the film delivers a strong social message about inclusion, equal opportunities, and respect for differently-abled individuals.

Moreover, the film is based on a real-life success story, making it more impactful and inspiring. It encourages viewers to challenge stereotypes and support a more inclusive society. Therefore, Srikanth is not only an entertaining film but also an educational and motivational one that carries an important message for contemporary India.
