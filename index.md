## Tips for Success

1. **Image Quality:** Use high-resolution images (at least 72 DPI for screen viewing)
2. **Consistency:** Keep image sizes and placement consistent across pages
3. **Readability:** Ensure text and images don't overlap; maintain white space
4. **Organization:** Follow the 5-page structure strictly for clarity
5. **Accessibility:** Use descriptive filenames and consider adding alt-text to images
6. **File Management:** Keep original images in a folder for easy reference and updates
